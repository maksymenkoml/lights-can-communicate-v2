# Rooms of Hue and Govee lights

Issue: #78. Follows #7 (`2026-11-22-any-light-set.md`). Precedes #60 and #59.

## Intent

The core knows one room: one desk layout and one Room group on the virtual
bridge. A home has several rooms with lights. The owner's home has three:

- **Owner's office**: a 32" monitor, the Sync Box, two Govee H6076 and four Hue
  lights. Sync runs here.
- **Second office**: a 27" monitor and Hue lights, among them a Lightstrip on
  the monitor. Its user runs the Hue Sync desktop app with her own Entertainment
  area on the physical bridge. She needs her lights in one zone with lighting
  presets; Sync there is not ours.
- **Bedroom**: one Govee light.

One physical Hue bridge and one Hue app account serve the home. The physical
bridge is in Apple Home, so every Hue light is already in Apple Home. Govee
lights reach Apple Home through the Homebridge plugin of this project.

## Goals

- The owner creates, renames and deletes rooms in Light Studio and places each
  connected light in one room.
- Each room has its own layout: placement, per-light corrections (scale,
  scene scale, Govee calibration, Sync role), optional desk and screen drawings.
- Each room is a Room group on the virtual bridge with its lights, so Hue app
  scenes on the virtual bridge apply per room to Hue and Govee lights, with the
  Light Studio corrections.
- Every connected light is in Apple Home for control and automations: Hue lights
  through the physical bridge, Govee lights through the plugin.
- Exactly one room is the Sync room: the room of the Sync Box. Sync, the
  Entertainment group of the virtual bridge and the paired native Entertainment
  area belong to it only.

## Non-goals

- Starting or stopping Sync from Light Studio as the normal path. Sync start and
  stop stay automatic through the Hue mobile app (Sync Box automation). The
  existing Sync Box controls in Light Studio stay as they are.
- Rooms or Room classes in Apple Home: Apple Home rooms are assigned by the owner
  in the Home app; no accessory can set them.
- More than one Sync room, or Sync for a second source (the Hue Sync desktop app
  streams to its own area on the physical bridge, without the core).
- Pairing with the physical bridge (#60) and the first-run flow (#59).

## Anti-goals

- The core changes an Entertainment area, room, zone or scene on the physical
  bridge that it does not own. In particular it never joins a light to, or
  removes a light from, an area that the Hue Sync desktop app uses.
- The Sync Box needs to be paired again: the Entertainment group "90" of the
  virtual bridge, its id, its six capture slots and its channels stay as they are.
- The current installation loses a light identity, a scene, a calibration or a
  HomeKit accessory.

## Constraints

- One physical bridge streams one Entertainment area at a time. While the Hue
  Sync desktop app streams, a Sync start of the core can be refused by the
  bridge, and the reverse. Light Studio says so in words.
- A Hue light belongs to one Room on a Hue bridge; the virtual bridge follows
  the same rule: a light is in exactly one room.
- Hue lights appear twice in the Hue app: once on the physical bridge and once
  as a virtual light on the virtual bridge. The owner accepted this.
- Light Studio corrections apply only where a command passes through the core:
  Sync, scenes of the virtual bridge, and Apple Home for Govee lights. A Hue
  light controlled from Apple Home or the Hue app on the physical bridge gets no
  correction.
- No backward compatibility: an older release need not read the new state;
  rollback restores the state snapshot (owner decision, 2026-11-24).
- Python runtime dependencies stay in the standard library.

## Approaches

1. **Rooms in Light Studio only.** A room switcher and one layout per room; no
   change to the virtual bridge. Presets come from Apple Home scenes. Smallest
   change, but Hue app scenes do not reach the Govee lights of other rooms.
2. **Rooms in Light Studio and on the virtual bridge (chosen by the owner).**
   As 1, and every room is a Room group of the virtual bridge, so Hue app scenes
   apply per room to Hue and Govee lights with the corrections.
3. **Rooms from the physical bridge.** Read the rooms of the physical bridge and
   mirror them. Rejected: Govee lights cannot be in those rooms, and the core
   would depend on rooms it does not own.

## Design

### Rooms and layouts

- A room has an id (`room-<hex>`), a name (1–40 characters) and a layout. The
  layout keeps the current version 7 shape per room (rows, screen, desk, zones),
  with the screen optional: a room without a screen has no `screen` object.
- A room index lists the rooms in order, names the Sync room and records the
  room of every connected light. The first start migrates the current
  `desk-layout.json` into the first room, named by its `room` field, which
  becomes the Sync room; every currently connected light joins it. Its virtual
  Room group is the existing group "1".
- The Sync room is fixed: it is the migrated first room, it cannot be deleted
  and the Sync designation does not move. The other rooms can be reordered for
  display.
- Every connected light has exactly one room, recorded in the room index,
  whether or not it has a row there. Connecting a light puts it in the current
  room; a light without a row keeps its room. A light row may appear only in the
  light's room.
- A room can be deleted only when it has no connected light: the owner moves or
  disconnects its lights first. Deleting it deletes its layout, including the
  rows of drawings and disconnected lights.

### Core and adapter

- The adapter reads every room. Per-light values (scene scale, scale, Govee
  calibration, names, Sync role, scene fade) come from the light's row in its
  room. The Sync profile is built from the Sync room only; a light outside the
  Sync room is never part of Sync.
- Scene fade (`sceneZone`) is per room, relative to that room's screen. A room
  without a screen has no scene fade.

### Connecting Hue lights per room

- A Hue light connected in the Sync room joins the paired native Entertainment
  area, as today (#7 stage 5b).
- A Hue light connected in any other room is connected for scenes only: it does
  not join any Entertainment area, it has no channels, and the core drives it
  through the CLIP API as it drives the migrated Hue lights outside Sync. This
  also connects a gradient light whose area channels would be shared (the
  Lightstrip of the second office).
- Moving a Hue light into the Sync room joins the area; moving it out leaves the
  area. Area membership follows the light's room and nothing else; the registry
  records the channels only while the light is in the area.
- The six capture slots of group "90" stay fixed whatever the area holds, as they
  do since #7: the Sync Box topology never follows the area membership.
- While the Hue Sync desktop app streams, the bridge ignores CLIP commands to
  the lights of its area; the core does not detect or queue them.

### Virtual bridge

- Each room is a Room group on the virtual bridge, named as the room, with the
  virtual lights of its connected lights. Group "1" stays the first room's
  group. A new room's group gets the lowest free id and a stable `id_v2` from its
  room id, so it keeps its id across restarts.
- A light moved to another room leaves its old group and joins the new one, and
  it leaves the scenes of its old room, as a Hue bridge does. A recalled scene
  therefore never drives a light of another room. Deleting a room deletes its
  group and the scenes of that group (the room has no connected light then).
- The virtual bridge restarts when the room set or membership changes, as it
  restarts today when the registry changes. Group "90" and its six slots do not
  change.
- While Sync is active, a change that restarts the virtual bridge or changes the
  area (add or delete a room, move or connect a light) is refused with the reason
  `sync_active`, as a light change is refused today. Renaming a room and editing
  a layout are allowed.

### Light Studio

- The room name in the header becomes a room switcher: the rooms in order, the
  Sync room marked, Add room, and per room Rename and Delete.
- Each room shows its own layout. The screen, the Sync zone and the Sync
  controls appear only in the Sync room. Other rooms may show a desk and a
  screen as drawings for placement, without Sync.
- Found lists discovered lights not placed in any room. Placing one adds its row
  to the current room; Connect connects it for that room (Sync area or scenes
  only, see above). Moving a light to another room is done from its card
  (Move to room) and keeps its corrections.
- The current-room choice is remembered per browser.
- Govee calibration and corrections work in every room; they do not depend on a
  screen. The Sync role control appears only in the Sync room.

## Stages

Each stage is a separate PR, verified live on the NAS before it is offered.

1. Rooms in the state and the adapter: room index, per-room layouts, migration
   of the current layout into the Sync room, per-light lookup across rooms, Sync
   profile from the Sync room. With one room, behavior is identical.
2. Scenes-only Hue lights: a Hue light outside the Sync room is connected and
   driven without an Entertainment area; moving between rooms joins or leaves
   the area.
3. Virtual bridge: one Room group per room, membership from the rows, stable
   group ids, restart on room changes.
4. Light Studio: room switcher, add, rename and delete rooms, per-room layout,
   Found per room, Move to room.

## Verification

- Offline: migration from the current state; per-room lookups; Sync profile
  unchanged for the Sync room; scenes-only Hue lights; group ids stable across
  restarts; Light Studio client tests per room.
- Live on the owner's NAS: create the second office with the Lightstrip on the
  27" monitor and the bedroom with its Govee light; Hue app scenes per room on the
  virtual bridge; every light in Apple Home; PS5 Sync in the owner's office
  unchanged; the Hue Sync desktop app in the second office unaffected.
