# Rooms of Hue and Govee lights

Issue: #78. Follows #7 (`2026-11-22-any-light-set.md`). Precedes #60 and #59.

## Intent

The core knows one room: one desk layout, one Room group on the virtual bridge,
and Sync configuration that is always shown. A home has several rooms with
lights, and a Sync Box belongs to one of them. The owner's home has three rooms:

- **Owner's office**: a 32" monitor, the Sync Box, two Govee H6076 and four Hue
  lights.
- **Second office**: a 27" monitor and Hue lights, among them a Lightstrip on the
  monitor. Its user runs the Hue Sync desktop app with her own Entertainment
  area on the physical bridge. She needs her lights in one zone with lighting
  presets; Sync there is not ours.
- **Bedroom**: one Govee light.

One physical Hue bridge and one Hue app account serve the home. The physical
bridge is in Apple Home, so every Hue light is already in Apple Home. Govee
lights reach Apple Home through the Homebridge plugin of this project.

Rooms are the unit the owner manages. A Sync Box is a device that the owner
registers in a room, like a light. Sync exists in a room only because a Sync Box
is registered there: the Sync configuration switch, the Sync zone and the Sync
roles appear only in that room.

## Goals

- The owner creates, renames, reorders and deletes rooms in Light Studio and
  puts each connected light in one room.
- Each room has its own layout: placement, per-light corrections, optional desk
  and screen.
- The owner registers the Sync Box in a room, and can move it to another room.
  Only a room with a Sync Box shows the Sync configuration switch and the Sync
  settings; its lights are the Sync lights.
- Each room is a Room group on the virtual bridge with its lights, so Hue app
  scenes on the virtual bridge apply per room to Hue and Govee lights, with the
  Light Studio corrections.
- Every connected light is in Apple Home for control and automations: Hue lights
  through the physical bridge, Govee lights through the plugin.

## Non-goals

- Starting or stopping Sync from Light Studio as the normal path. Sync start and
  stop stay automatic through the Hue mobile app (Sync Box automation). The
  existing Start/Stop control stays, in the Sync Box's room.
- Apple Home rooms: the owner assigns them in the Home app; no accessory can.
- Two Sync Boxes syncing at the same time (one physical bridge streams one area
  at a time). This design registers one Sync Box; the room model keeps the Sync
  Box per room, so a second box can be added later.
- Sync for the Hue Sync desktop app: it streams to its own area on the physical
  bridge, without the core.
- Pairing with the physical bridge (#60) and the first-run flow (#59).

## Anti-goals

- The core changes an Entertainment area, room, zone or scene on the physical
  bridge that it does not own. In particular it never joins a light to, or
  removes a light from, an area that the Hue Sync desktop app uses.
- Moving the Sync Box between rooms makes the owner pair it again in the Hue
  app: the virtual Entertainment group "90" that the box streams to, its id and
  its capture slots stay as they are.
- The current installation loses a light identity, a scene, a calibration or a
  HomeKit accessory.

## Two links of a Sync Box

A Sync Box has two separate links, and rooms touch only the second one:

1. **The stream.** In the Hue app the box is paired with the virtual bridge and
   streams to its Entertainment group "90" (the sampler, see the #7 design). The
   sampler's capture positions do not depend on which lights exist or in which
   room they are. Rooms never change this link.
2. **Control and placement.** Light Studio finds and pairs the box for
   Start/Stop (`.sync-box.json`), and now also records the box's room. The room
   decides which lights the stream drives.

The stream arrives whether or not the box is registered in a room. Without a
registered box no room has Sync, and Light Studio says that a Sync stream
arrives without a room.

## Constraints

- One physical bridge streams one Entertainment area at a time. While the Hue
  Sync desktop app streams, a Sync start of the core can be refused by the
  bridge, and the reverse. Light Studio says so in words.
- A light belongs to one Room on a Hue bridge; the virtual bridge follows the
  same rule.
- Hue lights appear twice in the Hue app: on the physical bridge and as virtual
  lights on the virtual bridge. The owner accepted this.
- Light Studio corrections apply only where a command passes through the core:
  Sync, scenes of the virtual bridge, and Apple Home for Govee lights. A Hue
  light controlled from Apple Home or the Hue app on the physical bridge gets no
  correction.
- The sampler's virtual lights (slots 1–6) are also the virtual lights of the
  six migrated lamps. A migrated lamp can be in a room without the Sync Box.
- No backward compatibility: an older release need not read the new state;
  rollback restores the state snapshot (owner decision, 2026-11-24).
- Python runtime dependencies stay in the standard library.

## Approaches

1. **Rooms in Light Studio only.** A room switcher and one layout per room; no
   change to the virtual bridge. Presets come from Apple Home scenes. Hue app
   scenes do not reach the Govee lights of other rooms.
2. **Rooms in Light Studio and on the virtual bridge (chosen by the owner).** As
   1, and every room is a Room group of the virtual bridge, so Hue app scenes
   apply per room to Hue and Govee lights with the corrections.
3. **Rooms from the physical bridge.** Mirror its rooms. Rejected: Govee lights
   cannot be in them, and the core would depend on rooms it does not own.

For Sync the owner decided: Sync belongs to the room where the Sync Box is
registered, not to a fixed room.

## Design

### Rooms, lights and the Sync Box

- A room has an id (`room-<hex>`), a name (1–40 characters), a layout and at
  most one Sync Box. A room index lists the rooms in order, the room of every
  connected light, and the room of the Sync Box.
- Every connected light has exactly one room, with or without a row there.
  Connecting a light puts it in the current room. A light row may appear only
  in the light's room.
- The first start migrates the current `desk-layout.json` into the first room,
  named by its `room` field; every connected light and the paired Sync Box join
  it. Its virtual Room group is the existing group "1".
- A room can be deleted only when it has no connected light and no Sync Box.
  Deleting it deletes its layout, with the rows of drawings and disconnected
  lights.

### Layout per room

- The layout keeps the version 7 shape per room: rows, desk, screen, scene zone
  and, in the Sync Box's room, the Sync zone.
- A room with the Sync Box needs a screen: registering the box in a room without
  one adds the screen at a default size for the owner to place. A room without
  the box may have a screen as a drawing for placement and scene fade.
- Sync settings (Sync zone, Sync fade, each row's Sync role and Sync scale) are
  kept in a room's layout when the box leaves it and apply again when it
  returns. They are hidden and unused while the room has no box.

### Core and adapter

- The adapter reads every room. Per-light values (scene scale, Govee
  calibration, names, scene fade) come from the light's row in its room.
- The Sync profile is built from the Sync Box's room only. Without a registered
  box there is no Sync profile and the stream drives no light.

### Hue lights and the native area

- The paired native Entertainment area holds exactly the Hue lights of the Sync
  Box's room. A Hue light in any other room is connected for scenes only: it is
  in no area, has no channels, and the core drives it through the CLIP API.
- Area membership follows the light's room and the box's room. Connecting a Hue
  light in the box's room, moving a Hue light into or out of it, and moving the
  box join or leave the area as needed.
- A light that cannot join the area (for example its gradient channels would be
  shared) stays connected for scenes and is shown as outside Sync with the reason.
  Today such a light is refused altogether.
- The six sampler slots of group "90" stay fixed whatever the area holds.
- While the Hue Sync desktop app streams, the bridge ignores CLIP commands to the
  lights of its area; the core does not detect or queue them.

### Virtual bridge

- Each room is a Room group on the virtual bridge, named as the room, with the
  virtual lights of its connected lights. Group "1" stays the first room's
  group. A new room's group gets the lowest free id and a stable `id_v2` from its
  room id.
- A light moved to another room leaves its old group and the scenes of its old
  room and joins the new group, as on a Hue bridge. A recalled scene never
  drives a light of another room. Deleting a room deletes its group and scenes.
- The virtual bridge restarts when the room set or membership changes. Group
  "90" does not change.
- A Sync stream to group "90" must drive only the lights of the Sync Box's room.
  A migrated lamp in another room must stay under scene and Apple Home control
  during Sync (verify against the pinned diyHue image; see Left to the builder).

### Changes during Sync

While Sync is active, a change that restarts the virtual bridge or changes the
area is refused with the reason `sync_active`: adding or deleting a room, moving
or connecting a light, and registering or moving the Sync Box. Renaming and
reordering rooms and editing a layout are allowed.

### Light Studio

- The room name in the header becomes a room switcher: the rooms in order, the
  Sync Box's room marked, Add room, and per room Rename, Reorder and Delete.
- The Sync configuration switch, the Sync zone, the Sync roles, the Sync scale
  and the Sync Box Start/Stop appear only in the room with the Sync Box. In the
  other rooms the layout edits the scene configuration only.
- The Sync Box appears in a room's object list. In a room without a box,
  "Add Sync Box" finds and pairs it, or moves the registered box to this room
  after a confirm.
- Found lists discovered lights not placed in any room. Placing one adds its row
  to the current room; Connect connects it in that room. "Move to room" on a
  light's card keeps its corrections.
- Govee calibration works in every room; it does not depend on a screen.
- The current-room choice is remembered per browser.

## Stages

Each stage is a separate PR, verified live on the NAS before it is offered.

1. Rooms in the state and the adapter: room index, per-room layouts, migration
   into the first room with the Sync Box, per-light lookup across rooms, Sync
   profile from the box's room. With one room, behavior is identical.
2. Hue lights per room: scenes-only Hue lights outside the box's room, area
   membership following rooms, a light that cannot join stays connected.
3. Virtual bridge: one Room group per room, membership and scenes per room,
   stable group ids, restart on room changes, the Sync stream limited to the
   box's room.
4. Light Studio: room switcher and room management, per-room layout, the Sync
   Box as a room device with Sync configuration only in its room, Found per
   room, Move to room.

## Verification

- Offline: migration from the current state; per-room lookups; the Sync profile
  of the box's room unchanged after migration; no Sync profile without a box;
  area membership on every move; group ids stable across restarts; Light Studio
  client tests per room, including the Sync controls only in the box's room.
- Live on the owner's NAS: create the second office with the 27" Lightstrip and
  the bedroom with its Govee light; Hue app scenes per room; every light in
  Apple Home; PS5 Sync in the owner's office unchanged; move the Sync Box to
  another room and back, with Sync following it; the Hue Sync desktop app in the
  second office unaffected.

## Left to the builder

- The room index file name and JSON shape, and the API routes for rooms.
- How diyHue sets a stable `id_v2` for a Room group (verify against the pinned
  image).
- The Hue Room class of a new room (a sensible default, such as Other).
- How the registry stores a scenes-only Hue light and how the native profile
  skips it.
- Whether diyHue changes the virtual sampler lights during a stream. If it
  does, so that a migrated lamp outside the box's room would follow the stream
  or ignore scenes, give the sampler its own virtual capture lights, which needs
  one Sync Box pairing in the Hue app, after the owner agrees.
